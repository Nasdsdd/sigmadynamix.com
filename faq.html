Futures Operator

Frequently asked questions

How we hire, how the trading works, and how operators are paid. If something is not covered here, write to support@sigmadynamix.com.

01

Selection process

We hire on execution alone. Every candidate takes the same live market simulation, and the ranking decides who moves forward — no CV, no interview, no referral.

STEP 01

Online assessment

A live market simulation in your browser. Three sessions of real NQ data, executed under time pressure.

≈ 30 minutes
STEP 02

Two-week evaluation

Daily sessions on the Terminal alongside the desk, under live conditions, closed by a full performance review at the end.

10 sessions
STEP 03

Live desk capital

You execute firm signals on firm capital. One 20-minute session a day, between 09:45 and 10:30 New York time.

Compensation starts

No fees to apply. No fees to trade. Fully remote.

Do I need trading experience or a CV? +
No. There is no CV and no interview. The assessment is the application: everyone sits the same simulation and is ranked on how they executed.
What happens if I am selected? +
A two-week evaluation on the Terminal first, under live session conditions, closed by a full review of your performance over the period. It confirms that what you showed in the assessment holds day after day. This evaluation period is not compensated — compensation starts when you move to live sessions on desk capital.
What equipment do I need? +
A desktop or laptop computer with at least 8 GB of RAM and a stable connection. The assessment cannot be completed on a phone or a small tablet — you are reading two price charts and placing orders under time pressure.
02

Trading mechanics

Do I get assigned to buy or sell? +
No. You choose on every signal. We send the signal, and you decide to buy or sell in the move that follows. Several operators trade the same move, each finding their own edge in it.
Do I need to analyse the market or build a strategy? +
No. The signal and its timing come from us. Your job is execution — entry, trade management, exit. It is scalping: you are in and out of the same move fast. We synchronise timing across operators and build teams trading it together.
How should I manage my entry and exit? +
There is no fixed pattern, only a signal to read. Two operators can trade the same signal differently, as shown below: one buys early into the dip and exits into strength, the other waits, sells once the move has run further, and covers on the pullback that follows.
How many points do operators typically aim to capture? +
Around 10–15 NQ points per trade, roughly $200–$300 per contract. If price keeps accelerating in your favour it is fine to let it run — moves of 20 to 30 points are not rare.
Can stop losses be customised? +
No. Stop losses are set by the company and cannot be modified by the operator. Every position carries the same automatic trailing stop.
03

Compensation

Variable compensation is 50% of net profits, from day one on the live desk. There are no fees and no desk charge.

MONTH 1
Guaranteed minimum
No minimum

Your first month on desk capital is pure profit split.

MONTH 2
Guaranteed minimum
$1,000/mo

If your split comes to less, the firm pays the difference.

MONTH 3 ONWARD
Guaranteed minimum
$2,000/mo

The floor every established operator keeps, month after month.

Compensation depends on operator performance. The guaranteed minimum tops up your profit split when it falls short for that month — it is a floor, not a fixed salary.

How and when will I be paid? +
By bank transfer at the beginning of each month. The first payment requires four full weeks of trading on desk capital: if you start late in the month, those weeks carry into the next one and you are paid at the beginning of the month after.
Are there any fees to apply or to trade? +
None. You never fund an account, and you never pay for data, software or desk access. You trade firm capital.
